# STRUCTURE — The Real Cost of Recruitment in 2026: The Numbers Behind the 2022 Figure Everyone Still Quotes

**Format:** data-benchmark-report · **Sections:** 10 · **Authored H3s:** 0 · **Boxes used:** 41


**The article's spine:** For a CHRO building or defending a hiring budget: the cost-per-hire figure most decks still quote is a stale edition of a SHRM number, and even the current one is a blended average that hides two axes the budget actually moves on, role band and hiring volume. This article dates every figure in circulation to its edition and statistic, then rebuilds the number from the invoiced hard costs (30-40%) and the uninvoiced soft costs (60-70%), so the reader leaves able to state a current, sourced benchmark, calculate their own segmented figure, and say exactly what it excludes.

**What it left out, and why:** None left out. Four of the five expected topics get their own section (formula in section 1, benchmarks in section 2, factors in section 4, the internal/external and hard/soft breakdown in section 6). The fifth, the definition of cost per hire, is folded into section 1's opening rather than given a section of its own, because a standalone definition section would repeat the formula section a reader would answer the same way.
