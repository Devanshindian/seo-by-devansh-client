The H1 and the intro

H1
9 Video Interview Platforms, Scored on Deepfake Defense
intro
Every ranking of video interview software scores the same things: workflow, AI analytics, and how neatly the tool plugs into your applicant tracking system. Not one of them rates whether the video interview platform can prove the person on camera is the candidate who applied. In a survey of 3,000 candidates, 6% admitted to interview fraud, either posing as someone else or sending a stand-in. That is the risk the buyer signs for, and the rating column nobody fills. The nine platforms below are rated on three checks: ID document check and face match, proctoring and browser lockdown, and deepfake or stand-in detection. Every rating comes from the vendor's own security and product documentation, nothing else.
quick answer
None of the nine covers identity from the application through to the final call. Talview and Jobma document the most: a government ID checked against a selfie, plus face matching. Willo and Interviewer.AI publish partial checks, and HireVue, Spark Hire and Hireflix document none, scoring the answer rather than the person. VidCruiter has the widest documented stack, but its fraud detection runs on recorded interviews only, so the live round stays open. No vendor here documents liveness testing to the recognized standard. Consent, bias audits and retention limits stay with the employer, whatever the vendor is certified for.

How We Scored Nine Platforms on Interview Integrity touch-up
• fixed re-intro: The intro now states the 6% fraud figure, so the section refers back to it instead of introducing it again.
Every video interviewing platform here is scored on what its own security page, help center, product documentation, and sales material state about candidate identity verification, as of August 2026 [c859]. Marketing category claims do not count. Where a vendor publishes nothing on an axis, the row records a documentation gap rather than a low score, because those are different findings for a buyer. An employer video interview platform, for this list, is one an employer, recruiter, or hiring manager buys, one that collects and scores real candidate interviews, and one priced per month, per interview, or by company size [c452]. General video conferencing is out, because it carries no verification, monitoring, or analysis layer at all [c2436]. The threat premise comes from Gartner, which projects that by 2028 one in four candidate profiles worldwide could be fake [c1]. That number moves budgets, so it deserves its counterweight. In The 6% who admitted to interview fraud came from Gartner's own survey of 3,000 candidates, 6% admitted to interview fraud, either posing as someone else or having someone else pose as them candidates [c3]. HireVue's research found no score drift in pre-employment assessments between 2022 and now [c648]. The distinction that makes a vendor claim readable is between assistance and impersonation. Assistance means the person on the call is the person who applied, getting help answering questions [c1379]. Impersonation means they are not. Assistance is a process design problem solved by better questions. Impersonation is a security problem that identity verification has to catch, and no redesign of the interview removes it [c1297]. Most teams have bought one tool and pointed it at both [c1377], and a policy that conflates them will over-flag honest candidates. Published rankings score neither. One review process that tested more than 80 video interview tools over four years weighted user-friendliness and integration most heavily, then separated close calls on employer branding and support quality [c249][c251]. Identity verification appears nowhere in that rubric. Even the compliance criterion recruiters are handed, SOC 2 and GDPR and consent management, is a data-protection claim rather than a proof-of-identity claim [c2352]. Buyer indifference is part of the reason: our own topline report with SHRM Labs found 63% of HR and talent acquisition practitioners do not look for proctoring capability at all [c8017].
