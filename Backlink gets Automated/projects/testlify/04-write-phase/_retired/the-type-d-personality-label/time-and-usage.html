How these numbers are produced

DataForSEO
The exact cost DataForSEO returns on every response. Not estimated.
DeepSeek
The exact token counts DeepSeek returns on every completion, priced at the published per-million rates. Cached prompt tokens are billed at a tenth of fresh ones, so a high cache-hit rate is money saved.
Claude / Codex
Not metered — they run on your subscription through the local CLI, so there is no per-call charge to record.
