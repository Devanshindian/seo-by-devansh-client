## Pricing & Plan Benefits

**Model**: Credit-based — pay only when a qualified candidate starts an assessment (one credit per candidate). Pay monthly or annually; annual billing saves ~30%. Base plan pricing is **locked for 24 months**, after which it moves to then-current pricing. Credits don't roll over and expire at the billing cycle's end; you can buy additional credits or upgrade/downgrade anytime.

**Trial & guarantee**: 7-day free trial, unlimited assessments, no credit card required, cancel anytime; **30-day money-back guarantee, no questions asked**. Non-profits get a **25% discount** (code TESTLIFYCARES25).

**Plan tiers** (annual pricing, all include unlimited assessments, all custom question types, all premium test libraries, brand appearance, and email support):
- **Starter** — from $99 → $69/mo; 100 candidate credits/year; 3 users; $10 per extra credit; early-stage startups.
- **Basic** — from $199 → $139/mo; 300 credits/year; 5 users; $7 per extra credit; growing businesses.
- **Business** — from $499 → $349/mo; 1,000 credits/year; 10 users; $5 per extra credit; scaling businesses; email + call support.
- **Premium** — from $999 → $699/mo; 3,000 credits/year; 20 users; $3 per extra credit; enterprise high-volume hiring; email + call support.
- **Unlimited** — $75,000/year for full access, unlimited credits (contact sales).
- **Custom, Enterprise, and Reseller** — custom pricing (contact sales).

**Standard vs Custom workspace**: white labeling, 100+ ATS integrations, additional user seats, and SAML SSO & 2FA are add-ons on Standard and included on Custom.

**Add-ons (annual)**: White label $2,388/yr; Integrations $2,388/yr (API, webhooks, ATS); extra users $180/user/yr. **Monthly**: White label $299/mo; Integrations $299/mo; users $15/user/mo. **Proctoring add-ons**: system check $0.25/check; Photo ID verification $0.50/attempt; Live video proctoring & screen recording $5/attempt. Candidate invites follow a fair-usage policy (1,000 monthly / 10,000 annual; $1 per additional invite). Currency: USD.
