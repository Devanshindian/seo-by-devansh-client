## Voice questions
Simple and effective: candidates record spoken answers to your custom prompts. Recruiters manually review responses to assess clarity, articulation, and how well candidates can express their thoughts.
Perfect for:
- Entry-level roles
- Multilingual screening
- Roles where tone and clarity matter
- Teams valuing authentic, human responses
## Voice AI (AI-powered audio interview)
An upgrade to traditional audio questions, Voice AI includes a smart AI interviewer that asks questions and analyzes responses in real-time. No need to schedule interviews or sift through hours of audio, let AI handle the heavy lifting while you get instant, structured insights.
Perfect for:
- High-volume hiring
- Sales and support roles
- Global screening
- Reducing unconscious bias
