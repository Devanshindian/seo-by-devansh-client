Criteria Corp is a comprehensive pre-employment testing platform that provides scientifically validated assessments to help companies hire smarter, faster, and more fairly.
Founded over 15 years ago, Criteria’s platform focuses on evidence-based talent decisions by measuring candidates across multiple dimensions: cognitive aptitude, personality traits, emotional intelligence, risk tendencies, and various job-related skills.
However the platform may not be the best fit for every team. If you’re looking for a platform with stronger candidate experience, conversational AI interviews, more flexible pricing, or advanced anti-cheating features, there are several strong alternatives to consider.
In this blog, we review the top 15 Criteria Corp alternatives, comparing their features, G2 ratings, and pricing to help you find the solution that best aligns with your team’s workflow, tech stack, and hiring goals.

- Extensive assessment library: Gain access to over 150 pre-built test templates tailored for roles in engineering, finance, support, and operations, ensuring comprehensive coverage across industries.
- Real-time scoring: Automatically categorize candidates into pass, borderline, or fail groups, allowing recruiters to quickly filter applicants and significantly reduce manual review time.
- Intelligent anti-cheating measures: All questions are crafted in-house, ensuring they aren’t publicly accessible. The Adaface team also validates each month whether tools like ChatGPT can solve the questions, ensuring the integrity of the assessments.
G2 Rating: 4.6/5
Pricing: Free trial available. Plans start at $180/year (Individual plan for basic needs) and scale up for larger teams

TestInvite is a flexible online exam and assessment platform built for secure, customizable testing across various use cases, including pre-employment screening, educational exams, and professional certification.
