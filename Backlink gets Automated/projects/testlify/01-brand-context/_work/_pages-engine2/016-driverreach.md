DriverReach is a web-based driver management software for trucking companies. It provides various features, including recruiting, hiring, compliance, and fleet management. “ATS integration” refers to integrating the DriverReach platform with an applicant tracking system (ATS). This would allow trucking companies to streamline the recruiting and hiring process by seamlessly integrating the two systems and eliminating the need to enter candidate information in multiple places manually.
The integration would also allow trucking companies to manage their entire driver workforce within a single platform, thus improving efficiency and data accuracy. By continually supplying cutting-edge technology, disseminating hiring best practices, and assisting CDL driver recruiters in taking critical measures to improve their marketing, recruitment, and retention efforts, DriverReach is at the forefront of this revolution. Our team has only begun to scratch the surface of what the industry will need to keep up with rising needs, thanks to various excellent relationships.
