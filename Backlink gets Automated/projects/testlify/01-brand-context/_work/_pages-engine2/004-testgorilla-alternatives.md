## Why companies no longer prefer TestGorilla
Many companies are rethinking their use of TestGorilla due to recurring challenges. Our research shows that these pain points are pushing HR teams to explore alternatives, with platforms like Testlify emerging as the preferred choice.
- Small test library: TestGorilla’s library has on the order of 400+ tests. In practice, companies needing specialized or niche assessments often run out of relevant options.
- Limited question types: TestGorilla only supports a few question formats (multiple-choice, essay, video response, and file upload). From a user perspective, this makes each assessment feel formulaic.
- Proctoring limitations: TestGorilla offers basic proctoring features such as full-screen tracking and multiple-attempt prevention. However, these features fall short compared to platforms such as Testlify, which offers AI-assistance detection, dual-camera proctoring, and more intelligent behaviour-tracking features.
- Escalating costs: Large enterprises seek tools that offer unlimited team seats, allowing everyone to access the platform without incurring extra costs. Here, TestGorilla’s pricing structure can be limiting as it charges based on the number of users or seats.
- Annual commitment required: Even the lowest plans on TestGorilla require an annual commitment, making it less than ideal for recruitment teams with tight budgets or seasonal hiring requirements.

### Features

- Cognitive assessment: Evaluates a candidate’s learning agility and problem-solving ability in areas like numerical, verbal, and abstract reasoning.
- Job targeting: Helps hiring teams define ideal behavioral and cognitive profiles for each role, improving job-fit scoring.
- Team discovery tools: Visualize team dynamics, identify strengths, and uncover areas of misalignment.
- Talent optimization platform: Goes beyond hiring to support onboarding, leadership development, employee coaching, and engagement programs.
### Pros
- Scientifically validated assessments for evaluating the behavioral traits and cognitive abilities of candidates
- Provides tools for creating detailed role profiles and visualizing team dynamics.
- Extends beyond recruitment into onboarding and coaching, supporting talent optimization.
- Ideal for strategic workforce planning, with a focus on long-term team success.
### Cons
- Does not test technical and role-specific skills, focusing mainly on traits and cognitive abilities.
- Implementation can be complex and time-consuming, better suited for experienced analytics teams.
- It may not be cost-effective for smaller organizations with limited budgets.
### What customers are saying
Reviews note PI is “highly customizable and valid” for predicting job performance. Customers appreciate how reports highlight candidate strengths/needs.
Drawbacks mentioned include “expensive pricing” and a huge learning curve when it comes to creating assessments.

eSkill is a long-established pre-employment testing platform known for its huge variety of skills tests. It offers both ready-made and custom assessments covering clerical skills, software proficiency, literacy and math, accounting, legal, call center scenarios, you name it.
### Features
- Massive test library: Choose from over 600 ready-to-use assessments or create custom tests by selecting subjects, topics, and questions tailored to your job or training needs.
- Simulations: Go beyond multiple-choice questions with eSkill’s interactive job simulation tests that evaluate how candidates perform in real-world job scenarios.
- Anti-cheating and advanced proctoring: Enables organizations maintain the integrity of assessments by monitoring behaviors such as copy-pasting and tab switching, automatically flagging any suspicious activity in real time.
- Reporting and analytics: Access detailed candidate reports, including overall scores and performance breakdowns by subject or question, allowing you to gain insights into each applicant’s strengths and weaknesses.
### Pros
- Vast library spanning technical, cognitive, safety, and even software simulation tests.
- Easily mix-and-match questions to build custom assessments.
- Strong analytics and score reports (skill-gap charts, detailed breakdowns).
- Good integrations (Greenhouse, iCIMS, etc.), and multilingual support.
### Cons
- Interface can be clunky: some users say it’s hard to find specific questions without searching around
- Fewer pre-built job-specific tests than promised (reviewers sometimes have to build exams from scratch).
### What customers are saying
Users emphasize eSkill’s flexibility and breadth. For example, one recruiter says it lets them “find the most useful questions for each department” by customizing tests.
However, some users point out that navigating the test library can be tedious and that they would like more ready-made assessment templates to save time. A few users found the pricing to be expensive for low-volume hiring.
In general, though, eSkill’s customer support and reporting tools are well regarded.

## 15. Canditech

- Job Simulations: Simulations for coding, Excel exercises, open-text responses, and more.
