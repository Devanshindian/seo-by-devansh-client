## What is HackerRank?
HackerRank is an enterprise-grade developer assessment platform and coding challenge hub that has been on the market for over a decade. It enables companies to create skill-based technical assessments to screen candidates effectively.
In addition to assessments, HackerRank offers a suite of products including FaceCode for live coding interviews and a platform for hosting hackathons, tapping into a global community of developers.
### How HackerRank works?
HackerRank equips recruiters with the tools to design, deliver, and analyze skill assessments that mirror real-world job demands. The platform ensures every step from building assessments to analyzing results is efficient, insightful, and aligned with real job requirements. Here’s how each part of the process works:
#### 1. Build the assessment
HackerRank allows recruiters and hiring managers to create custom assessments by choosing from its extensive library of over 7,500 pre-built questions. Users can also build role-specific tests by selecting predefined templates tailored to various technical positions.
#### 2. Invite candidates
Once your assessments are created, you can invite candidates directly from HackerRank’s platform, or via mail. HackerRank supports bulk invites, meaning you can send out assessments to a large group of candidates with just a few clicks. Candidates will receive an invitation link to access the test, where they can start the assessment directly without needing to create an account.
#### 3. **Review results**
After the test is completed, HackerRank’s detailed candidate report gives recruiters insight into how well each candidate performed. The platform provides a breakdown of the candidate’s scores for each individual problem, the time they spent, the number of test cases passed, and even video recordings for live coding interviews.
#### 4. **Conducting live interviews**
For later-stage evaluations, HackerRank offers CodePair, a collaborative coding environment for live technical interviews. During these sessions, candidates can solve problems while the interviewer tracks their progress in real time.
#### 5. Data compliance
HackerRank is fully GDPR and ISO 27001 compliant ensuring the secure, ethical, and transparent handling of candidate data. These compliance measures demonstrate HackerRank’s commitment to protecting personal information and maintaining the highest standards of data privacy and security.
