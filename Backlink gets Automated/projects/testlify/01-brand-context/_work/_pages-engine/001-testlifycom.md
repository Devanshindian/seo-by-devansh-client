## Frequently asked questions (FAQs)
Want to know more about Testlify’s skills assessment tool? Here are answers to the most commonly asked questions about our company.
### What is Testlify?
Testlify is a skills assessment and interviewing platform that helps companies hire top talent quickly, accurately, and affordably. Recruiting at scale is complex, time-consuming, and risky, and we take the stress out of finding the best candidates with deep analysis that’s accurate, automated, and unbiased.
### What is pre-employment testing software?
Pre-employment testing software is a powerful tool that recruiters can use to analyze prospective employees quickly and efficiently. Testlify’s pre-employment tests have been carefully created with the specific intention of matching your needs, so you get just what you need!
Our vast test library of 3,500+ tests cover all the recruitment tests, so we have unique tests for you no matter what your company’s requirements.
### Why is pre-employment testing important?
Pre-employment testing is an important part of the employee selection process. It helps companies find candidates with relevant skills, saving time and money on training costs and increasing productivity. At Testlify, we advocate the use of pre-employment testing software primarily for:
- Testing on-the-job skills
- Identifying the best talent 3x faster
- Building a diverse pool of candidates
- Championing a data-driven HR culture
### How does Testlify help in my hiring process?
Testlify’s AI-powered pre-hire assessments help companies measure the skills and job fitment of a candidate. These tests allow for quick screening of candidates, eliminate bias in hiring, thereby increasing the productivity of recruiters and hiring managers. Our unique assessment technology is a proven solution against high employee turnover rates.
### How are the pre-employment tests created?
Testlify’s pre-employment tests are built by subject matter experts and I/O psychologists, validated psychometrically to ensure they accurately measure skills and predict job performance. Randomized question banks prevent answer sharing, while bias reviews ensure every candidate is assessed purely on merit — helping teams hire faster and more confidently.
### What are the different types of questions asked in the pre-employment assessment test?
The questions would depend on the industry and job role for a specific online assessment. They could include MCQs (Multiple Choice Questions), descriptive questions, video-based questions, audio questions, open-ended questions, typing tests, file upload, Google doc, sheet and slides questions, programming questions and live coding challenges.
### What are the different types of tests on Testlify?
Testlify has Role Specific Tests, Language Tests, Programming Tests, Software Skills Tests, Cognitive Ability Tests, Situational Judgment Tests, CEFR Test, Typing Test, Coding Test, and Psychometric Tests.
### How is Testlify different from other pre-employment tools?
Testlify is a pre-hire assessment platform built for TA teams who need to screen faster, reduce bad hires, and make defensible hiring decisions. Our assessments are validated by I/O psychologists and built to withstand EEOC scrutiny.
- Our assessment removes unconscious bias from your screening process and finds hidden gems from underrepresented backgrounds.
- We prioritize on-the-job skills over trick questions to find the most qualified candidates.
- Non-googleable questions
- Advanced proctoring
- Video response questions (to have a 360-degree view of candidates)
- Low-stress, adaptive tests, which take just 30 mins to complete.
- A fun, engaging, and quick assessment process that ensures minimal drop-off rates throughout your recruitment process.
