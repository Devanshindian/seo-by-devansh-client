## Frequently asked questions (FAQs)
Want to know more about Testlify’s skills assessment tool? Here are answers to the most commonly asked questions about our company.
### What is Testlify’s AI Resume Screener?
Testlify’s AI Resume Screener automatically analyzes resumes pulled from your ATS. It scores candidates based on how well they fit the job. This helps you quickly find and shortlist the best talent.
### How does the AI determine candidate fit?
The AI checks candidates based on the criteria you set. It gives clear match scores and fit labels like High, Medium, or Low.
### Can I automate candidate shortlisting and assessments?
Yes! You can set thresholds to automatically shortlist or reject candidates and trigger assessments for those who meet your criteria.
### Does your resume screening tool integrate with my ATS?
Absolutely. Testlify pulls resumes from your ATS and pushes back both match scores and assessment results for a seamless workflow.
### Can I customize screening criteria for different roles?
Yes. You can define role-specific requirements, set cut-off scores, and adjust thresholds to match your hiring needs.
### How does Testlify ensure fairness and reduce bias?
The AI applies consistent, role-based criteria across all resumes, standardizing evaluation and reducing human bias in shortlisting.
### How secure is candidate data?
Testlify adheres to enterprise-grade security standards to ensure your candidate data remains safe and compliant. Our platform follows GDPR, CCPA, and NY Bias Law rules. It also meets SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 standards. This ensures privacy and data protection at every step.
### How many candidates can Testlify screen?
Testlify scales effortlessly, screens thousands of resumes every month without slowing down your recruitment process.
### Can I see the scores and fit labels before making a decision?
Yes. All match scores and fit labels are transparent and explainable, giving you complete visibility before advancing candidates.
### How quickly can we start using the AI Resume Screener?
You can get started in days, not weeks. Our team will help set up integrations, thresholds, and workflows to get your screening running fast.
