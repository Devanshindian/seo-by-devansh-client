What types of questions can I ask in a video interview on Testlify?
You can ask questions related to personal description, work experience, aspirations, strengths and weaknesses, and challenging work scenarios.
Can I customize the video interviews to fit specific job roles?
Yes, you can customize the questions to fit the job roles to assess the candidate’s skills and competencies.

The time limit is customizable, and you can set a limit on how long the candidate has to respond.

You can create standardized questions to ensure all candidates receive the same evaluation and eliminate bias.

Yes, Testlify’s video interviews can seamlessly integrate with your existing ATS to streamline the hiring process.
