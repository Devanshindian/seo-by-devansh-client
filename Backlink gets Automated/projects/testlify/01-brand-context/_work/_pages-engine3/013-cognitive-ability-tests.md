## Frequently asked questions (FAQs)
Want to know more about Testlify? Here are answers to the most commonly asked questions about our company.
How is cognitive ability testing used in the hiring process?
Assessments that test cognitive ability are generally used to evaluate candidates as part of the pre-screening process. These assessments evaluate various cognitive aptitudes, problem-solving skills, critical thinking skills, working memory, and more.
The feedback from these tests can give organizations the necessary data to compare and select the best talent in an organized way that can greatly minimize the time needed to select the best candidates.
Can cognitive testing predict a candidate’s workplace performance?
Testing cognitive ability can help predict whether a candidate will be able to succeed in the workplace. Our tests can evaluate a candidate’s ability to learn, adapts to challenging work environments, find solutions, make decisions, and more.
What does a cognitive assessment consist of and what does it measure?
Testlify offers cognitive ability assessments that evaluate various aptitudes including numerical reasoning, verbal reasoning, logical reasoning, spatial ability, and working memory.
What is the duration of cognitive ability tests?
Each test, and you may use several are about 10 minutes in duration each. You can customize the assessment to test for more skills which will affect the total time of an assessment.
Are the cognitive ability tests available to view?
You will not be able to view the test unless you sign up. If you would like more information feel free to use the chatbot to discuss or send us any questions you might have to support@teslify.com.
How do I customize tests?
Assessments can be customized by adding any additional skill test from our library of tests. These can be a combination of aptitude, language, programming, software, or role-specific skills.
Why test a candidate’s cognitive ability?
Finding the best talent for your organization requires not only candidates with the required qualifications but also the aptitude for the position. Testing an applicant’s cognitive ability to solve problems will give you deeper insights into their actual ability and help your selection process.
This will ultimately increase your recruitment team’s ability to compare candidates with real-time scoring and insights that go beyond what they see on resumes.
Are cognitive ability tests reliable?
Our Cognitive aptitude tests are highly accurate and have been tested to ensure their results are consistent even when taken again. Cognitive tests help minimize the complexity of testing candidates, minimizing the resources needed and providing powerful insights for organizations to make informed decisions.
