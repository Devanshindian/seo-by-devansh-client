## Frequently asked questions (FAQs)
Want to know more about Testlify? Here are answers to the most commonly asked questions about our company.
What skills can I test under situational judgment tests?
Based on the job role you are testing, you can test for the following situational judgment tests: Business judgment, Business communication, Time management, Business negotiation, Critical thinking, and Business ethics.
How can I customize tests?
Any new skill test from our test library can be added to assessments to make them more unique. Just add the assessments that are relevant to the job role.
Are the situational judgment tests reliable?
Our situational judgment tests are extremely accurate, and they have been examined to verify that the results are reliable even after repeated administrations.
Can I add multiple skills to my custom assessment?
Absolutely, yes. Your job description will be used to create a custom assessment that will ask questions about all the essential abilities you’ve listed.
Does every candidate get the same questions?
Yes, it makes it much simpler for you to evaluate candidates. The MCQ options and question order are both randomized. We have proctoring and anti-cheating systems in place.
