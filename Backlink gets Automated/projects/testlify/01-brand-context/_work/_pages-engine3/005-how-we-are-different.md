## Frequently asked questions (FAQs)
### Can I integrate Testlify with my existing applicant tracking system (ATS)?
Testlify offers seamless integration with popular ATS platforms, allowing you to manage assessments within your familiar workflow.
### What industries can benefit from Testlify’s skill assessment tool?
Testlify’s versatility makes it suitable for a wide range of industries, from technology, SaaS, recruitment and finance to healthcare.
### What measures does Testlify take to prevent cheating during remote assessments?
Testlify incorporates a lot of anti-cheating features including web cam access, location access, screen recording and more to maintain the integrity of the assessment.
### Can I create my own assessment questions from scratch?
Yes you can create customized questions that align with your company’s specific requirements and the role you’re hiring for.
### How does adaptive testing help in evaluating candidates more accurately?
Adaptive testing tailors the difficulty of questions based on candidates’ responses, providing a more precise measure of their skill levels and minimizing the likelihood of skewed results.
