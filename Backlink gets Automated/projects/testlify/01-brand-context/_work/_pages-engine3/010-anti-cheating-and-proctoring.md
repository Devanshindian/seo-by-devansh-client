## See how proctoring features works
Ensure assessment integrity with advanced AI-powered proctoring. Monitor behavior, detect violations, and maintain fairness at scale.
## Session recording and webcam snapshots
Our platform records the candidate’s entire test session, including real-time webcam snapshots at intervals. This creates a verifiable audit trail that helps confirm identity, detect impersonation, and provide visual context for any flagged behavior.
- Build trust in your hiring decisions
- Great for validating remote test-taking integrity
- Helps resolve disputes or candidate challenges
## Location access & IP address tracking
With IP and geolocation tracking, you can verify that assessments are taken from authorized locations. This helps prevent proxy test-taking and outsourced assessments.
- Identify suspicious access or geographic mismatches
- Block unauthorized access or VPN masking
- Adds a powerful layer of authentication
## Copy/paste blocking & screen switch detection
Our system disables copy-paste functionality and tracks any attempt to switch tabs or windows during the test. You can set tolerance limits and automatically terminate sessions if violations occur.
- Enforces strict exam-like discipline
- Prevents external answer sourcing
- Keeps candidates focused on the test
## Question randomization
Testlify automatically randomizes questions and answers to reduce the risk of answer sharing or memorization. You can even pull from question pools to create thousands of unique combinations.
- Makes cheating nearly impossible
- Encourages individual performance
- Improves fairness across the board
## AI assistance detection
Our AI monitors test behavior and analyzes answers for signs of ChatGPT, code generators, or AI writing tools. You get notified when we suspect AI-assisted responses.
- Protect the credibility of technical tests
- Stay ahead of modern cheating trends
- Red-flag potentially fabricated work
## Snapshot proctoring
Enable interval-based webcam snapshots to see the candidate’s environment at multiple points. It’s a lightweight but effective way to catch unauthorized behavior.
- Easy to set up
- Reduces the need for full live proctoring
- Adds accountability for the entire session
## Full-screen & forced full-screen mode
By enforcing full-screen mode, candidates are locked into the assessment interface. Exiting or minimizing the window can trigger warnings or auto-termination.
- Minimizes distractions and side-searching
- Increases engagement and integrity
- Useful for all test types
## Mouse-out & tab proctoring
Monitor how long a candidate’s mouse is outside the test area or how often they switch tabs. Set tolerance levels and auto-terminate based on severity.
- Perfect for high-stakes roles or tests
- Detects focus loss and suspicious actions
- Customize thresholds based on your risk tolerance
## Honesty agreements & private invites
Require candidates to agree to a customized honesty declaration before starting the test. Combine that with private invites to limit access and boost compliance.
- Creates a sense of responsibility
- Prevents test link sharing
- Adds professionalism and control
## Question-level activity logs
See how long each candidate spent on every question, if they switched tabs during specific answers, and whether their behavior aligned with expectations.
- Enables data-driven decisions beyond just scores
- Spot inconsistencies in performance
- Identify rushed or skipped questions
## Advanced AI identity verification
Using facial recognition and AI image analysis, we match the candidate’s live camera feed against their submitted ID or profile image for real-time identity verification.
- Fully automated for scale and speed
- Stops impersonation and ghost test-taking
- Ideal for remote hiring and contractor screening
## Make screen sharing mandatory
Require candidates to share their screen for the entire duration of the test. Monitor all activity and catch cheating attempts instantly.
- Simple to enable, hard to cheat
- Works well for whiteboard interviews and coding tasks
- Adds transparency to technical skill validation
## Set your own violation tolerance
Every organization is different, so we let you define your own thresholds.
- Tab-switching tolerance (in seconds)
- Mouse-out limits
- Violation counts before auto-termination
- Custom honesty agreements
