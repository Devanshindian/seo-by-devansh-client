# Testlify — Brand Scope

*The one-page reference every asset-idea agent reads before turning a competitor page into an idea. Direction, not a rulebook. When the page in front of you clearly says something this scope doesn't, trust the page and note it.*

## 1. What we are / who we serve

Testlify is a talent-assessment platform. It sells skills assessments and conversational AI interviews (chat, voice, and video) for pre-employment screening, backed by a 3,500+ test library, 20+ proctoring measures, and 100+ native ATS integrations. The buyer is a recruiter or talent-acquisition lead, from a ten-person startup to an enterprise TA function, who is drowning in applications and measured on time-to-hire, cost-per-hire, and quality-of-hire. The job they hire us for: stop guessing from resumes and gut feel, and screen people on proven skills fast, fairly, and in a way that survives a legal audit.

## 2. Subjects we can credibly own

These are examples of the kind of thing we own, not a checklist and not the full set. If you read a page and find a strong on-brand subject that isn't listed here, use it. Treat this as the shape of our territory, not its fence line.

- **Skills-based hiring over resumes** — the core conviction. Why demonstrated skill beats credentials and gut feel.
- **Pre-employment testing and assessment design** — test types, validity, adverse-impact, EEOC-defensible selection.
- **AI interviewing** — chat, voice, and video screening; scoring communication and soft skills at volume.
- **Anti-cheating and proctoring** — remote-test integrity, AI-assistance detection, the honest trade-offs.
- **Hiring at scale and high-volume/frontline recruiting** — throughput, funnel drop-off, campus drives.
- **Role- and industry-specific hiring** — technical, blue-collar trades, finance, healthcare, retail, BPO.
- **The recruiter's craft** — interview questions, hiring processes, scorecards, time-to-hire, cost-of-a-bad-hire.
- **Fairness, bias, and compliance in hiring** — reducing bias, audit trails, NYC LL144 and EU AI Act readiness.
- **Internal mobility and skills-gap development** — promoting and reskilling on the same evidence bar.

Anything a recruiter or HR leader would search while trying to hire better is likely in our world.

## 3. Off-limits / not us

Examples of subjects outside our world. Directional signal, not a hard ban. A cue here is a strong hint to skip the subject or transplant its format, not an automatic override of what the page actually is.

- **Payroll, benefits administration, and HRIS record-keeping** — adjacent HR, but not our job.
- **Post-hire performance management, engagement surveys, and OKRs** as ends in themselves.
- **General B2B SaaS, marketing, or sales advice** unconnected to hiring.
- **Pure developer-tooling / coding-education content** aimed at engineers learning to code (we test coders, we don't teach coding).
- **Job-seeker career advice** (resume-writing, interview prep for candidates) — we serve the hiring side, not the applicant.
- **Deep legal or immigration guidance** beyond hiring-compliance framing a lawyer would sign.

## 4. Transplant note

When a competitor wins links on a subject that sits outside our world but does it with a strong **format** (a calculator, a benchmark report, a template library, an interactive quiz, a ranked directory), keep the winning format and point it at our world. Rebuild the format around a hiring/assessment subject we credibly own. Record the original off-brand subject in Notes so the trail is traceable, and never let that original subject become our subject. We borrow the shape, not the topic.
