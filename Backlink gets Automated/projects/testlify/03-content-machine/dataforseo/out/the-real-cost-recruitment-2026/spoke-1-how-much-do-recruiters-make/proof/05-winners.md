### What the winners cover — how much do recruiters make

**Confirmed format:** Definitional/deep-guide, split into a Q&A-style breakdown of pay by employment type, plus (on one page) narrative case examples; deep — gogpac ~14.6k words, Kondo ~12.0k words, Betterleap ~6.3k words.

**Common H2s (most competitors have):**
- Agency vs. in-house recruiter pay compared side by side
- Salary vs. commission structure explained
- Per-placement / per-hire commission specifics
- In-house recruiter pay broken out separately
- Commission models walked through in detail

**Where the winners drift:**
- gogpac pivots twice into unrelated blog content (AI trainers, interview tips, remote onboarding) and into a "become a recruiter at gpac" recruitment pitch, neither of which answers the pay question.
- Betterleap's headings are dominated by a repeated "meet your recruiting agent" CTA, reading more like a product pitch than a pay breakdown.

**Gaps we can own:**
- Retained-search recruiter pay (retainer %, milestone/upfront splits) — absent from all three heading sets.
- RPO recruiter pay model — not named on any page.
- Role- and seniority-specific dollar ranges in a structured table rather than anecdote (Kondo covers career stage but through narrative "stories," not $ ranges).
- A single table comparing per-hire pay across agency/contingency, retained, RPO, and in-house — no page structures the comparison this way; Kondo only splits agency vs. in-house, gogpac splits salary vs. commission but not by engagement model.
- Sourced commission percentage benchmarks (e.g., 20% vs. 25% contingency fee) tied to public industry-fee data rather than a single site's claim.
