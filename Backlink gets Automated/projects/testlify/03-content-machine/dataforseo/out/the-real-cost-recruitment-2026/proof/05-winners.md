### What the winners cover — cost per hire

**Confirmed format:**
- Mixed: definitional/how-to guide (Juicebox: definition → cost breakdown → formula → benchmarks → factors → reduction) is the fullest structure; SHRM runs as an editorial/news piece; Metaview runs as a ranked diagnostic listicle ("eight leaks"). Deep — Juicebox ~6.3k words, Metaview ~7.6k, SHRM ~8.1k.

**Common H2s (most competitors have):**
- Definition of cost per hire
- Formula / how to calculate it
- Benchmarks and average cost figures
- Breakdown of what counts as a cost (internal vs. external, hard vs. soft)
- Factors that drive cost per hire up or down
- Ways to reduce cost per hire
- Business impact of high cost per hire (productivity, mis-hires)

**Where the winners drift:**
- SHRM pivots into recruiter/HR emotional burnout ("The Emotional Toll") — adjacent wellbeing angle, not cost accounting. Metaview frames its entire piece around its own product's diagnostic ("Manual, generic AI, Metaview: the cost stack compared") rather than a neutral benchmark.

**Gaps we can own:**
- No winner segments cost per hire by role/job type (all three keep the breakdown generic — internal vs. external, or leak-by-leak — never by role).
- No winner segments cost per hire by hiring volume (high-volume vs. low-volume hiring cost structures are absent from every heading list).
- No winner anchors on a current-year public benchmark figure as a standalone, cited data point — Juicebox's "Benchmarks and average cost" section exists but headings alone don't confirm data recency, so this is the piece's clearest edge over the aging 2022 SHRM figure this page currently cites.
