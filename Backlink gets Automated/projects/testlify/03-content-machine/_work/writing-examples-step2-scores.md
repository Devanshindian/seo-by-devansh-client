## Why 6 failed (recurring reasons, all judged only vs brand-voice.md)
- **No proof** — generic adjective-driven claims, no real numbers/cited sources (violates *Confident, Backed by Proof*).
- **Skills-First thesis missing** — generic HR-explainer content that never ties to skills-based hiring.
- **Third-person / not "you"** — encyclopedic register instead of *The Helpful Hiring Expert* speaking to the recruiter.
- **Banned AI-clichés + anti-example shape** — inventory-Qs and 4-pillars literally open like brand-voice.md's own ❌ "Not Testlify Voice" example ("In today's rapidly evolving…", "delve," "unlock," "elevate").
- **Broken clarity/grammar** — several had copy errors, violating the "rewrite if it needs a second read" rule.

## The pattern
The one winner is a **"how a famous company hires at scale" case study** — which is literally the ✅ example inside brand-voice.md. Testlify's **generic HR glossary/explainer blog content is off-voice**; the on-voice content clusters in specific genres (scale case studies; and per the brand-voice evidence, competitor comparisons like vervoe/maki/testgorilla were confident + proof-backed too).
