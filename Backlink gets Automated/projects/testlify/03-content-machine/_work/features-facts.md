### FAQ
- Q: What does a LockDown Browser do? A: A LockDown Browser restricts access to other apps, websites, and screen functions during an online exam. It creates a secure, full-screen testing environment to prevent digital cheating.
- Q: How safe is the LockDown Browser? A: LockDown Browsers are generally safe to use, but they take complete control of the system during exams. While secure, users should only install from official sources and review the privacy policies.
- Q: Does the LockDown Browser track location? A: No, a LockDown Browser does not track your physical location. It monitors on-screen activity only. Location tracking would require additional permissions or tools, which most do not use.
- Q: How do browser lockdown and screen recording deter test fraud? A: Browser lockdown blocks new tabs, copy-paste, screenshots, and external apps, which removes the means to cheat during an assessment. Screen recording captures continuous video of the candidate's screen, which reduces the incentive to cheat.
- Q: Can a LockDown Browser track cheating? A: Not directly. It blocks cheating tools and monitors screen use, but it doesn't detect behavior. To track cheating, it's often combined with webcam monitoring or AI proctoring.
