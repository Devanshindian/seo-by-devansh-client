What are gamified assessments? Gamified assessments and EEOC compliance Gamified assessments are subject to the same federal legal standards as any other selection tool. The EEOC Uniform Guidelines on Employee Selection Procedures apply to any assessment used to make or influence hiring decisions, regardless of format. “It’s a game” is not a compliance defense. Adverse impact requirements The four-fifths rule applies. If your gamified assessment produces a selection rate for any protected group below 80% of the rate for the highest-selected group, you have a prima facie adverse impact case. This does not make the assessment illegal — it means you must demonstrate job-relatedness and business necessity. For assessments with behavioral gameplay mechanics, the adverse impact analysis should go beyond pass/fail rates and examine whether specific game mechanics — reaction-time tasks, interface familiarity, device requirements — introduce construct-irrelevant variance that correlates with protected group membership.
