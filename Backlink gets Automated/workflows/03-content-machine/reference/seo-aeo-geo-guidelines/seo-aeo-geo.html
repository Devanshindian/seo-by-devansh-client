SEO / AEO / GEO — the per-article checklist

The gate every article passes before it publishes. SEO = ranking on Google. AEO = getting cited in AI answer boxes. GEO = getting cited by AI assistants like ChatGPT. Most items overlap — write well for one and you cover all three.

Content Structure Keywords Links AEO / GEO Sources E-E-A-T Meta Readability Voice Ship

01Content

Content
Length matches the natural range for the format — no padding to a word count target
Primary keyword identified · 3–5 secondary + semantic terms chosen
Unique value vs competitors · factually accurate + current · no thin content · duplicates canonicalised

02Structure

Structure
One H1, ≤60 characters, with primary keyword near the start and benefit-focused
4–7 H2s (≥2 with keyword variations, ≥50% as questions) · H1 → H2 → H3 hierarchy only
Answer the main question in the first 100 words · include a TL;DR summary block above the fold
40–60 word self-contained answer under each question H2 · FAQ with 5 Q&As · close with forward CTA, not recap
No horizontal dividers in the body — use heading structure to separate sections
Soft check — include a data/comparison table if the topic naturally fits one

03Keywords

Keywords come from the research keyword-set (primary · variations · secondaries · in-body) handed down by the research phase.

Keywords
Primary keyword in H1 + first 100 words + 2–3 H2s + conclusion · secondaries used where mapped
All variations woven in naturally · no stuffing · no density-% target

04Links

Links
≥3 internal links · varied descriptive anchors · less than 30% exact-match · never "click here" · max 2 per paragraph
One internal link to a product/feature page where it fits naturally
≥2 external links to credible primary sources (internal: dofollow · citations: nofollow · sponsored: marked)
No competitor cited · every link returns 200

05AEO / GEO

AEO = Answer Engine Optimisation (getting into AI answer boxes on Google). GEO = Generative Engine Optimisation (getting cited by ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude). Both reward the same thing: self-contained, precise, citable content.

AEO / GEO
Direct 40–60 word answer under each question H2 · at least 50% of H2s are questions · 4+ inline citations
Quotable self-contained sentences · precise numbers/dates/entities · one idea per section · ≥1 embedded video where it adds context
Bonus (not a gate) — at least one original or proprietary stat

06Sources & Claims Integrity

Sources & Claims
Every stat → its exact source URL · URL returns 200 · year matches · verified on the page (not from memory)
2–3+ distinct source domains · max 2 citations per domain · cite-don't-promote
Cite the most credible and most recent source available
Self-consistency: every number reconciles

07E-E-A-T

Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness — Google's quality signals for content. These are the markers that tell search engines and readers the article is written by someone who knows the topic.

E-E-A-T
Theme-native author box with named author + credentials · never inside the body · datePublished + dateModified both visible
First-hand markers in the body · citation integrity verified

08Meta

Meta
Title 50–60 characters with keyword · description 140–160 characters with keyword + value + CTA · slug has keyword · all unique + intent-matched

09Readability & Images

Readability & Images
15–20 word sentences · 2–4 sentence paragraphs · 80%+ active voice · a subheading every ~350 words
Images relevant + compressed · keyword-aware alt text ≤125 characters + descriptive filenames

10Voice & Authority

Voice & Authority
Brand voice maintained (brand-voice.md) · written in the chosen persona's lens
Authority honesty: product positioned only where it genuinely wins · differentiation via real POV or illustrative use-case · no fake customer/quote/metric

11Ship

Ship gate
Overall score ≥ 80 · no P0 failures · avoid-AI-writing final pass run
P0 failures — these fail the article regardless of score Fabricated stat or metric · missing or broken source URL · named/implied fake customer or quote · missing author box · competitor cited as a source
