Avoid AI Writing — remove machine-generated patterns before ship

Audit every article before publishing to strip AI-isms (machine-generated word choice, sentence structure, formatting). Applied as the final gate — no draft ships without this pass.

Concept Top patterns Words to avoid The gate

01The one rule

Certain word choice, sentence shapes, and formatting patterns are statistically more common in LLM output. Remove them. The piece must read human — not generic vendor — before it publishes.

  • Signals, not proof. These patterns appear in human writing too (deadline pressure, non-native writers, technical genres). Use as a quality filter, not an accusation.
  • One final pass. Run this AFTER seo-aeo-geo.md ships. It's the last gate before publishing.

02Top patterns to strip

Formatting

  • Em dashes (— or --): Replace with commas, periods, or rewrite as two sentences. Target: zero per article.
  • Bold overuse: Max one bolded phrase per major section, or none. If it's important, lead the sentence with it instead.
  • Emoji in headers: Remove entirely. No "🚀 What This Means".
  • Excessive bullet lists: Convert to prose paragraphs unless it's genuinely step-by-step or comparative.

Sentence structure

  • "It's not X — it's Y": Rewrite as a direct positive statement. Max one per piece.
  • Hollow intensifiers: Cut genuine, real (as intensifier), truly, to be honest, let's be clear. Just state the fact.
  • Vague endorsement: Cut worth reading, worth your time, worth exploring. Say why it matters instead.
  • Hedging: Cut perhaps, could potentially, it's important to note that. Make the point directly.
  • Compulsive rule of three: Vary groupings. Use two, four, or a sentence instead of always three items.

04The gate — before publishing

Every article gets one pass. No exceptions. This is part of the SEO / AEO / GEO ship gate (same as P0 failures).

  • Run this skill over the final draft
  • Remove or replace every flagged pattern
  • Re-read for flow after editing — some replacements need sentence restructuring
  • Ship only after this pass is clean
Non-negotiable An article that passes seo-aeo-geo.md but fails avoid-ai-writing is NOT ready. The "human writing" gate is as hard as the "source integrity" gate. No exceptions.
