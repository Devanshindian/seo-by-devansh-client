## Intro
- [ ] **The intro is 2 to 4 sentences and states the dataset immediately** — what was studied, how big it is, why it matters.
- [ ] **Careful:** the original wording said "states N / dataset size", which reads as though we ran a survey. We do not. We gather published figures. Whatever this becomes must describe the sources, never imply original research.

## Key-findings menu
- [ ] **A numbered key-findings menu near the top, before any detail.** It is the citation menu, and usually the single most-linked part of the page.

## Close and takeaways
- [ ] Close with takeaways plus citation aids: an embeddable chart and a ready-made "cite this" line.
